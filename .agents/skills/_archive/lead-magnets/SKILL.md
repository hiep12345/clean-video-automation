## Before Planning

**Check `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` first.**

Gather context (ask if not provided):
1. **Business**: Company, ICP, problems solved
2. **Current Lead Gen**: Current capture methods, conversion rate
3. **Content Assets**: Existing content to repurpose, expertise, internal tools/templates
4. **Goals**: List growth / lead quality / product education? Buyer stage? Timeline?

## Lead Magnet Principles

1. **Solve a Specific Problem** — one clear pain point, not a broad topic
2. **Match Buyer Stage** — awareness=education, consideration=evaluation, decision=implementation
3. **High Perceived Value, Low Time** — looks worth paying for, consumable in <30 min
4. **Natural Path to Product** — solves a problem your product also solves
5. **Easy to Consume** — one format, works on mobile, no special software

## Gating Strategy

| Approach | When | Trade-off |
|----------|------|-----------|
| **Full gate** | High-value, bottom-funnel | Max capture, lower reach |
| **Partial gate** | Preview + full version | Balance reach & capture |
| **Ungated + optional** | Top-funnel education | Max reach, lower capture |
| **Content upgrade** | Blog post + bonus | Contextual, high-intent |

**Form fields**: Email-only = highest conversion. Every extra field reduces conversion 5-10%.

**Framing**: Make value obvious, show preview, add social proof ("Downloaded by 5,000+"), reduce risk ("No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.")

## Output Format

### 1. Lead Magnet Recommendation
- Format, topic, target buyer stage, why this format, estimated effort

### 2. Content Outline
- Key sections, length/scope, unique value

### 3. Gating & Capture Plan
- Gate approach, form fields, landing page structure

### 4. Distribution Plan
- Promotion channels, content upgrade opportunities, paid amplification

### 5. Measurement Plan
- KPIs and targets, what to A/B test first
