# Tiêu chuẩn và Best Practices — Human Resources

## 1. Confidentiality
- Thông tin lương, performance, disciplinary là STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL.
- Chỉ share thông tin với authorized parties.
- Tuân thủ data protection regulations.

## 2. Onboarding
- 30-60-90 day plan cho mọi nhân viên mới.
- Day 1: equipment, accounts, welcome, buddy assignment.
- Week 1: team introductions, process overview.
- Month 1: clear expectations + first deliverable.

## 3. Performance Review
- Feedback phải specific, measurable, actionable.
- Format: Situation → Behavior → Impact → Next Steps.
- Tránh recency bias: review cả period, không chỉ recent events.

## 4. Compensation
- Compare với market data (percentile 25th, 50th, 75th).
- Internal equity: same role + same experience = similar pay.
- Total compensation = base + bonus + equity + benefits.
