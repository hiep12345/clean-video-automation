## YouTube Title Formulas

**Formula 1: Question hook**
`This [Animal] [shocking behavior] — Here's Why`

**Formula 2: Superlative**
`The [Most/Deadliest/Strangest] [Thing] You've Never Seen`

**Formula 3: Stakes**
`[Animal] [Does Thing] — And It Works On Humans Too`

**Good:** "This Centipede Hunts Bats Mid-Flight — Here's How"
**Bad:** "Amazing Science Facts About Giant Centipedes!"
