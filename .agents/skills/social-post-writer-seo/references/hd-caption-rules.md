# Home Decor (HD) Caption Rules

Use these rules for every Home Decor Reel produced with
`home-decor-editorial-v1`.

## Content promise

- Lead with the visible room transformation or the single design decision.
- Describe only objects, materials, lighting, and actions visible in the final
  approved video.
- Keep the tone calm, editorial, practical, and specific.
- Do not invent product brands, prices, affiliate links, room dimensions, or
  claims about professional results.
- Do not describe the AI generation workflow in the hook.

## Facebook

- One short hook plus one useful observation or question.
- Use no more than three topical hashtags total, including `#HomeDecor`, plus
  the required content-ID hashtag.
- Add `AI-generated visual` as a plain-language disclosure.
- Publishing is manual-only.

## Instagram

- One short hook, one design takeaway, and one direct engagement question.
- Use no more than five topical hashtags, including `#HomeDecor`, plus the
  required content-ID hashtag.
- Add `AI-generated visual` as a plain-language disclosure.
- Publishing is manual-only.

## Guardrails

- Captions must match the L3-approved final file and its established object
  count.
- Never claim that a generated room is a real client project or completed
  renovation.
- Never add a shopping or affiliate call to action unless a verified destination
  and explicit user authorization are both present.
- Drive, Notion, and Facebook actions remain disabled for local production
  batches.
